❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about Medica platform

How do I take a doctor appointment on Medica? ▼
You can browse the Appointments page or Doctors Directory, select your desired doctor, and click the Take Appointment button. It will open the dedicated booking form where you select your appointment date, available time slot, and reason for consultation.
Where can I view, reschedule, or cancel my appointments? ▼
All your booked appointments are stored in your Profile Dashboard under the 📋 My Appointments section (located directly above Medical Profile). You can click 📅 Reschedule to choose a new time or ❌ Cancel with a reason.
How do I search for medicines and generic names? ▼
Visit the Medicines page to search over 50,000 indexed drugs by brand name, generic formulation, or medical category. Each medicine detail page displays usage guidelines, dosages, side effects, and cheaper alternative recommendations.
Can I purchase medicines and track orders on Medica? ▼
Yes! You can add medicines to your cart, specify delivery information, and complete home delivery checkout. Track your order history and live statuses (Pending, Processing, Shipped, Delivered) directly in your Profile Dashboard.
What is the AI Health Assistant and how does it work? ▼
The AI Health Assistant allows you to describe your health symptoms in English or Bangla. The intelligent model provides primary health advice, emergency warnings, and recommends which doctor specialty you should consult. Note: AI advice is for informational guidance and does not replace in-person medical diagnosis.
How do medicine reminders work? ▼
Open Reminders to create custom dosage reminders with specific times, dosage counts, and frequency. Medica sends automated browser notifications when it's time to take your medication.
Is my personal and medical profile information secure? ▼
Yes, Medica prioritizes user data privacy. Password information is securely hashed using bcrypt encryption, and session authentication utilizes encrypted JWT tokens.
What emergency medical helpline numbers should I call in Bangladesh? ▼
In emergencies, call 16263 (Health Window / স্বাস্থ্য বাতায়ন) for 24/7 doctor teleconsultation, 999 for National Ambulance & Emergency, or 333 for Government Information.
